# Stellar photosphere image

`stellar-photosphere-v2.png` is an AI-generated illustrative surface texture, created with the built-in image generation tool (generate mode). It is not a telescope observation or a spectral-class-specific physical surface simulation. The renderer tints the luminance pattern and packs the image into each Blender project.

Final prompt:

Use case: scientific-educational. Asset type: seamless 2:1 equirectangular UV surface texture for a luminous star in a Blender educational animation. Generate a detailed realistic stellar photosphere texture, filling the entire rectangular image edge to edge, with fine densely packed convection granules, delicate luminous plasma filaments and a few subtle darker active regions. Neutral silver-white grayscale luminance map with visibly defined granules and medium contrast, to be tinted blue-white or golden by the shader. Flat even illumination across the whole map, seamless left and right edges and no shading baked into the texture. This is a surface map, NOT a rendered sphere: no horizon, no black space, no stars in the background, no corona, no planet, no craters, no landmasses, no text, no watermark. High detail, natural irregular fine-scale solar granulation, wide 2:1 aspect.
